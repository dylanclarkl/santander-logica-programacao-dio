# 📝 Class 03: Comparison (Relational) Operators

## 📌 Main Concepts
Comparison operators are used to compare two values and always return a boolean result: **`true`** or **`false`**. They are essential in game development to evaluate rules, such as checking if a player's level is high enough to access a dungeon, verifying item rarity, or checking if an account is banned.

---

## 🛠️ Summary of Comparison Operators

* **Equal to (`==`)**: 
    Compares only the **value** of two operands.
* **Strict Equal to (`===`)**: 
    Compares both the **value and the data type/format** of the operands. This is the recommended standard in modern JavaScript.
* **Not equal to (`!=`)**: 
    Checks if values are different (compares values).
* **Strict Not equal to (`!==`)**: 
    Checks if value and/or format are different.
* **Greater than (`>`)**: 
    Checks if the left operand is greater than the right one.
* **Less than (`<`)**: 
    Checks if the left operand is lesser than the right one.
* **Greater than or equal to (`>=`)**: 
    Checks if the left operand is greater than or equal to the right one    .
* **Less than or equal to (`<=`)**: 
    Checks if the left operand is lesser than or equal to the right one.
